Privacy Policy

Jaguares MTB Publisher

Last Updated: July 2026

Jaguares MTB Publisher is an internal application developed to manage and publish content to the official social media accounts of the Jaguares MTB community.

Information We Collect

The application only accesses the information required to authenticate with supported social media platforms and publish approved content.

This may include:
  • OAuth access tokens
  • Refresh tokens
  • Public profile information required by the platform
  • Content selected for publication

How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating users.
  • Publishing approved content.
  • Managing social media integrations.
  • Maintaining application security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties except when required to interact with the official APIs of supported social media platforms.

Data Security

Access credentials are securely stored and protected using industry-standard security practices. Only authorized administrators have access to the application.

Data Retention

Authentication tokens are retained only as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality or until revoked by the user.

Your Rights

Users may revoke application access at any time through the corresponding social media platform.
