Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Jaguares Social Automation is a private application used exclusively to administer the official Facebook Page of Jaguares MTB Yucatán.

Information We Process

  • Facebook Page access tokens.
  • Administrator identifiers required by Meta.
  • Member names and birthday information managed by the organization.
  • Photographs voluntarily provided for publication.
  • Publication identifiers and operational logs.

Purpose

Information is processed only to publish authorized content, manage birthday greetings, ride announcements, photo albums and approval workflows.

Sharing

No personal information is sold or shared with third parties. Information is transmitted only to Meta using the official Graph API to publish approved content.
